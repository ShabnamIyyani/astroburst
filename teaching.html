In the classroom

Teaching

On teaching

Teaching is a deeply enlightening experience for the teacher. It significantly enhances the clarity of thought and understanding for the teacher. There are moments in teaching that are so overwhelmingly fulfilling, they affirm the choice of teaching as the right career.

On learning

Learning in my perspective is entirely a profound personal experience. It gives one the opportunity to enrich the very fundamental nature of human: curiosity and wonder. While a teacher can provide a recipe for a topic, it is the student who needs to try out that recipe and taste the dish to finally transform the information into understanding and eventually into one's own knowledge.

I hope every student cherishes the journey of learning and remains a life long learner.
